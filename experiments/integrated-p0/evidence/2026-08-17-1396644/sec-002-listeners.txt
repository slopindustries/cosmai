first two say how this session arrived; the third says no other route existed.

Recorded by the API itself at startup, not asserted here. Every api.started

`-k sec_002` asserts that it is a loopback address, that a non-loopback
COSMA_API_HOST is refused rather than corrected, and that no non-loopback
address of this host can reach it.


Why this is not the full enumeration
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SEC-002 asks for recorded output rather than a summarised claim, and the first
capture of this file met that by committing the whole lsof listing. That
listing named unrelated processes on this machine, their pids, and the
loopback ports they use — host state that is not this project's data and that
`docs/conventions/data-handling.md` gives no basis to redistribute.

The narrowing is not a filtered listing, which would be the summarised claim
the scenario warns against. It is a narrower question whose answer is complete:
"how many sockets are listening" and "how many of them are PostgreSQL" are both
answered in full, and the counts make a false negative visible. A reviewer who
wants the enumeration itself runs the command above on their own host, where
the same two numbers are reproducible and the process names are their own.

Retention: this file is the record. The full listing is not retained anywhere.
